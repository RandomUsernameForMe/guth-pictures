# Shoot Approaches Framework

Produce **three distinct** concepts per client. Distinct = different on the axes below, not
three flavors of the same idea. Each must be justified by something specific in `research.md`.

## Differentiation axes
- **Energy:** still/composed ↔ dynamic/in-motion
- **Proximity:** intimate close ↔ environmental wide
- **Setting:** studio/seamless ↔ real location/workplace ↔ outdoor
- **Light:** soft/clean ↔ hard/dramatic ↔ natural/ambient
- **Tone:** authoritative ↔ approachable ↔ aspirational/editorial

## Starter archetypes (adapt, don't copy)
- **Editorial Authority** — magazine-grade, controlled light, strong posture; for thought
  leaders, founders raising, premium positioning.
- **Approachable Founder** — warm, candid, environmental, eye contact; for trust-led B2C,
  coaches, service brands.
- **Dynamic In-Action** — motion, working, gesture, shallow DOF; for makers, operators,
  creatives.
- **Architectural / Brand-World** — subject within considered space, lines & negative space;
  for design-forward or premium brands.
- **Lifestyle Aspirational** — light, airy, context of the good outcome; for lifestyle,
  wellness, consumer brands.

## Each concept must specify
1. **Name** + one-line pitch
2. **Why it fits** — cite a research finding
3. **Mood** — 3–5 adjectives
4. **Color palette** — 3–5 hex values (feed these to `build-board --palette`)
5. **Wardrobe** — looks, textures, do/don't
6. **Location / setting** — specifics + backup
7. **Lighting** — key/fill/rim, modifiers, mood
8. **Shot list** — 6–10 frames (crop + purpose, e.g. "tight headshot for LinkedIn")
9. **Pose direction** — posture, energy, hands, gaze
10. **Image search terms** — phrases to feed `WebSearch` in Phase 4/5

Keep the three balanced enough that any one could be chosen, but clearly different in feel.
