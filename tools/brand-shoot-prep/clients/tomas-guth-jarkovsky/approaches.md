# Shoot Approaches — Tomáš Guth Jarkovský

Three distinct concepts, each mapped to one facet of his brand. Together they form a modular
set usable across Amnesty / advocacy, party & speaking, and AI-teaching contexts.

---

## Concept A — "Humanist Advocate"
**Pitch:** Documentary-style portraits that read as principled, warm, and real — Amnesty / NGO register.

- **Why it fits:** advocacy specialist at Amnesty + writes on war/human rights; his audiences
  distrust gloss and reward authenticity (research: brand voice, NGO visual norms).
- **Mood:** humanist, candid, warm, quietly serious — 4 adjectives.
- **Palette:** `#1c1b19` `#5b5347` `#a8967a` `#e7e1d6` `#b23a2e` (warm neutrals + one humane red accent). Strong **b&w** option.
- **Wardrobe:** simple, textured, lived-in — knit, open collar, no flashy logos; earth tones.
- **Location / setting:** real environments — desk with papers, bookshelf, window light, street/urban exterior. Backup: home/office near window.
- **Lighting:** natural / available light, soft directional from window; documentary, not staged.
- **Shot list:** (1) tight candid headshot, eyes to camera; (2) ¾ at desk, mid-thought;
  (3) by window, looking out; (4) walking/urban environmental; (5) hands + notebook/laptop detail;
  (6) talking/gesturing as if mid-conversation.
- **Pose direction:** relaxed, unguarded; weight settled; genuine micro-expressions over posed smiles; hands engaged.
- **Image search terms:** "documentary portrait window light", "NGO advocate environmental portrait", "black and white candid headshot natural light", "photojournalistic professional portrait desk".

---

## Concept B — "Modern Civic"
**Pitch:** Clean, confident portraits with architectural restraint — political / speaking / LinkedIn register.

- **Why it fits:** Pirate Party deputy (foreign affairs), civic/public figure; political visual
  norms = clean, confident, restrained palette (research: competitive landscape).
- **Mood:** principled, composed, contemporary, trustworthy.
- **Palette:** `#0d0f12` `#2b2f36` `#8a9099` `#f2f3f5` `#1f6feb` (graphite + clean blue accent; black aligns with Pirate visual language).
- **Wardrobe:** sharp but unfussy — dark jacket, fine knit or crisp shirt, no tie; minimal.
- **Location / setting:** studio seamless (mid-grey) OR modern civic architecture — concrete, glass, strong lines, negative space.
- **Lighting:** soft clean key + subtle rim; even, controlled; slight contrast for authority.
- **Shot list:** (1) classic centered headshot, direct gaze; (2) ¾ arms-folded, confident;
  (3) full-body against architectural line; (4) negative-space wide for cover/quote overlay;
  (5) profile/looking-off "vision" frame; (6) seated, leaning in.
- **Pose direction:** upright, grounded stance; shoulders open; calm direct eye contact; controlled, deliberate.
- **Image search terms:** "modern political portrait clean studio", "architectural environmental portrait negative space", "confident professional headshot grey backdrop", "civic leader portrait minimal".

---

## Concept C — "Technologist / AI Educator"
**Pitch:** Contemporary, dynamic, in-context frames — tech credibility & AI-instructor register.

- **Why it fits:** software PM / game designer + self-described AI instructor + MFF UK informatics;
  tech-educator visual norms = contemporary, teaching/stage context (research: hats, gap to exploit).
- **Mood:** sharp, modern, engaged, forward — energetic vs. A/B.
- **Palette:** `#0a0e14` `#142433` `#1f3a5f` `#3ddc97` `#e8edf2` (deep tech navy + cool teal/green accent, screen-glow friendly).
- **Wardrobe:** smart-casual contemporary — clean shirt or fine knit, modern, uncluttered.
- **Location / setting:** teaching/stage, modern workspace, in front of screen/whiteboard; cool ambient + accent.
- **Lighting:** modern mixed — soft key + cool practical/screen glow as accent; some motion-friendly.
- **Shot list:** (1) mid-gesture explaining/teaching; (2) at laptop, focused; (3) presenting to (implied) audience;
  (4) ¾ with screen/whiteboard bokeh behind; (5) dynamic hands-in-motion; (6) approachable smile to camera.
- **Pose direction:** dynamic, gestural, in-the-moment; hands active; engaged energy, not static.
- **Image search terms:** "tech speaker portrait stage", "AI instructor teaching candid", "modern professional with laptop cool lighting", "conference presenter dynamic portrait".

---

### Recommendation
Default to boarding **all three** — they're a complementary modular set. If picking one to lead:
**B (Modern Civic)** is the most broadly reusable hero look; **A** for Amnesty/advocacy; **C** for AI-teaching.
