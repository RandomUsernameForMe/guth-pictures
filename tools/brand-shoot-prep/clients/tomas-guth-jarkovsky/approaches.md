# Shoot Approaches — Tomáš Guth Jarkovský

Three distinct concepts, reweighted to his current identity (AI-led). Together a modular set:
the **AI-educator / technologist** look leads (commercial engine), a **humanist-advocate** look
serves Amnesty / NGO, and a **modern operator** look covers product/PM, speaking, LinkedIn.
Each concept carries its **own** toggle palettes.

---

## Concept A — "Modern Technologist / AI Educator"
**Pitch:** Contemporary, engaged, in-context frames — AI-instructor & tech-credibility register. His lead commercial identity.

- **Why it fits:** headline is "AI-Powered PM & Product Owner"; sells AI workshops + consulting to
  schools & firms; MFF UK informatics + game design. Tech-educator visual norms = contemporary,
  teaching/stage/workspace context (research: AI is now the lead hat).
- **Mood:** sharp, modern, engaged, forward — the most energetic of the three.
- **Palety:**
  - **Nokturno:** `#0a0e14` `#142433` `#1f3a5f` `#3ddc97` `#e8edf2` (deep tech navy + cool teal accent, screen-glow friendly)
  - **Studio Light:** `#f4f5f7` `#d9dee4` `#8a929c` `#2b3340` `#2f6df0` (clean bright, blue accent — workshop/teaching brightness)
  - **Grafit:** `#101317` `#23282f` `#5c646e` `#c7ccd2` `#e0573a` (neutral graphite + warm accent — modern but human)
- **Wardrobe:** smart-casual contemporary — clean shirt or fine knit, modern, uncluttered.
- **Location / setting:** teaching/stage, modern workspace, at screen/whiteboard; cool ambient + accent.
- **Lighting:** modern mixed — soft key + cool practical / screen glow as accent; motion-friendly.
- **Shot list:** (1) mid-gesture explaining/teaching; (2) at laptop, focused; (3) presenting to (implied) audience;
  (4) ¾ with screen/whiteboard bokeh behind; (5) dynamic hands-in-motion; (6) approachable smile to camera.
- **Pose direction:** dynamic, gestural, in-the-moment; hands active; engaged energy, not static.
- **Image search terms:** "tech speaker portrait stage", "AI instructor teaching candid", "modern professional with laptop cool lighting", "conference presenter dynamic portrait".

## Concept B — "Humanist Advocate"
**Pitch:** Documentary-style portraits that read principled, warm, real — Amnesty / NGO register.

- **Why it fits:** Amnesty advocacy specialist; writes on war/human rights; his NGO audiences
  distrust gloss and reward authenticity (research: brand voice, humanist visual norms).
- **Mood:** humanist, candid, warm, quietly serious.
- **Palety:**
  - **Hřejivá:** `#1c1b19` `#5b5347` `#a8967a` `#e7e1d6` `#b23a2e` (warm neutrals + one humane red accent)
  - **Dokument B&W:** `#111111` `#3a3a3a` `#777777` `#bcbcbc` `#ececec` (monochrome documentary)
  - **Okenní světlo:** `#201d1a` `#6e5f4d` `#c2ab86` `#efe9dd` `#7a8b6f` (soft daylight neutrals + muted sage)
- **Wardrobe:** simple, textured, lived-in — knit, open collar, no flashy logos; earth tones.
- **Location / setting:** real environments — desk with papers, bookshelf, window light, urban exterior.
- **Lighting:** natural / available light, soft directional from window; documentary, not staged.
- **Shot list:** (1) tight candid headshot, eyes to camera; (2) ¾ at desk, mid-thought;
  (3) by window, looking out; (4) walking/urban environmental; (5) hands + notebook/laptop detail;
  (6) talking/gesturing as if mid-conversation.
- **Pose direction:** relaxed, unguarded; weight settled; genuine micro-expressions over posed smiles.
- **Image search terms:** "documentary portrait window light", "NGO advocate environmental portrait", "black and white candid headshot natural light", "photojournalistic professional portrait desk".

## Concept C — "Modern Operator"
**Pitch:** Clean, confident portraits with architectural restraint — product/PM, speaking, LinkedIn hero.

- **Why it fits:** software PM / product owner + civic public figure; product/operator visual
  norms = clean, confident, restrained, modern-minimal. Broadly reusable hero look (research:
  product/PM landscape; party role now faded, so civic-modern not hard-political).
- **Mood:** principled, composed, contemporary, trustworthy.
- **Palety:**
  - **Grafit + modrá:** `#0d0f12` `#2b2f36` `#8a9099` `#f2f3f5` `#1f6feb` (graphite + clean blue accent)
  - **Beton:** `#15171a` `#3f4248` `#9a9ea4` `#e6e7e9` `#c08a4a` (architectural greys + warm brass accent)
  - **Čistá světlá:** `#fbfbfc` `#e3e6ea` `#9aa1a9` `#33383f` `#1f6feb` (high-key minimal, blue accent)
- **Wardrobe:** sharp but unfussy — dark jacket, fine knit or crisp shirt, no tie; minimal.
- **Location / setting:** studio seamless (mid-grey) OR modern architecture — concrete, glass, strong lines, negative space.
- **Lighting:** soft clean key + subtle rim; even, controlled; slight contrast for authority.
- **Shot list:** (1) classic centered headshot, direct gaze; (2) ¾ arms-folded, confident;
  (3) full-body against architectural line; (4) negative-space wide for cover/quote overlay;
  (5) profile/looking-off "vision" frame; (6) seated, leaning in.
- **Pose direction:** upright, grounded stance; shoulders open; calm direct eye contact; deliberate.
- **Image search terms:** "modern professional portrait clean studio", "architectural environmental portrait negative space", "confident headshot grey backdrop", "product manager portrait minimal".
